#  Space Exploration
 
ok so this is a little 3D game where you drive a tiny rover around the Moon and find all the stuff humans have left up there. like, actual real stuff. landers, rovers, even some crash sites. 

 
i got the idea from [messenger.abeto.com](https://messenger.abeto.com) (go look at it, it's so pretty) but i wanted to do a **space version** instead of a town one.

##  What is this?
 
You're a small rover on a small Moon. there are glowing beacons floating over different spots, and each one is a real thing that was sent to the Moon and left there. drive up to one, **click it**, and you get its whole story:
 
-  when it was left there
-  which country sent it
-  where it is on the Moon (real coordinates!)
-  what happened to it
-  a random fun fact
there are **21 relics** total. can you visit them all?
 
---
 
##  Features
 
-  a low-poly Moon with craters, rocks, and the Earth chilling in the sky
-  drive around with dust flying out behind your rover
-  footprints and wheel tracks left at the Apollo sites (yes they're still there irl)
-  a counter so you can see how many you've found (beacons turn green when you visit them)
-  rover sounds and little chimes (you can mute it, i won't be offended)
-  works on phones too, there are on-screen buttons
---
 
## Controls
 
-W or up arrow to go forward 
-S or down arrow to go back
-A or left arrow to turn left
-D right arrow to turn right 
-Shift to boost 

 
On mobile just use the arrow buttons in the corner.

## The relics
 
some of the stuff you can find:
 
- **Luna 2** (1959) – first thing humans ever crashed into the Moon
- **Luna 9** (1966) – first soft landing
- **Surveyor 1** and **Surveyor 3** (1966, 1967)
- **Apollo 11, 12, 14, 16, 17** landers
- **Apollo 15 Lunar Rover** – the first car on the Moon 🚗
- **Lunokhod 1 and 2** – robot rovers from the USSR
- **Yutu and Yutu-2** (China, including the far side!)
- **Chang'e 5**
- **Beresheet** (Israel, crashed lol)
- **Chandrayaan-2** (crashed) and **Chandrayaan-3** (landed!) from India
- **SLIM** (Japan, landed upside down but still worked)
- **Odysseus** (USA, tipped over)
